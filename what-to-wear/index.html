What to Wear - Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 22, 2026

Overview

What to Wear is a weather-based clothing recommendation app. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

Data stored on your device

The following data is stored locally on your device and never leaves it:

  • Clothing rules you create (labels, temperature ranges, icons)
  • Photos you take of clothing items
  • Your selected location name
  • App preferences (e.g. onboarding status)

Data sent to third parties

The app sends your selected location's coordinates (latitude and longitude) to Open-Meteo, a free weather API, to fetch current weather data. No personal identifiers are sent with this request.

When you search for a location, your search query is sent to Open-Meteo's geocoding API to find matching locations.

No other data is sent to any third party.

What we do NOT collect

  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No advertising or ad identifiers
  • No crash reporting
  • No user accounts or authentication
  • No device identifiers
  • No GPS or device location (location is entered manually)

Camera

The app uses your camera only to photograph clothing items. Photos are processed and stored locally on your device. They are never uploaded or shared.

Background processing

Google ML Kit is used on-device to remove backgrounds from clothing photos. This processing happens entirely on your device - no images are sent to Google or any server.

Children's privacy

This app does not knowingly collect any data from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
